### Added
- TypeScript extractor: `new <X>Client(...)` (ts-proto / `@grpc/grpc-js` generated clients) is a gRPC client call site when `<X>Client` is imported in the file; it becomes a `CallsRpc` edge only when `<X>` or `<X>Service` is a gRPC service declared in the graph. Module-level constructions (`const client = new AdServiceClient(...)`) are attributed to a synthetic node named after the binding. `otel-demo` golden score: 87.5% / 53.8% -> 100% / 100% precision/recall.
- `.github/workflows/golden.yml`: golden-corpus precision/recall ratchet in CI (online-boutique, bank-of-anthos, otel-demo at 1.0/1.0).

### Fixed
- The standard gRPC health service (`grpc.health.v1.Health`) is no longer resolved as a service-to-service `CallsRpc` target (spurious `checkout -> health` edge in `otel-demo`).
