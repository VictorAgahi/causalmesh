### Fixed
- `scripts/determinism.sh` (the CI determinism gate) no longer fails intermittently and silently on loaded runners. It piped `mesh-mcp graph --format fingerprint` (4 lines of output) into `head -n 1`; when `head` exited between the binary's writes, `mesh-mcp` got `EPIPE`, exited 1 with its stderr sent to `/dev/null`, and `pipefail` + `set -e` ended the script with exit 1 and no output (macOS CI run 36267673943). The output is now captured whole to a file, every failing run prints its command, exit code and stderr, background runs are awaited by pid (a bare `wait` used to hide their failures), and an `ERR` trap names any other failing command. A relative `MESH_MCP_BIN` is resolved before the runs `cd` into each workspace, `SEQUENTIAL_RUNS`/`CONCURRENT_RUNS` are validated (non-negative integers, at least 2 runs in total), and two workspaces with the same basename no longer share result files. Works under bash 3.2 and bash 5.
