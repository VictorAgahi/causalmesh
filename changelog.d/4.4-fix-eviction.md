### Fixed
- Index cache eviction no longer empties a cache that is less than two batches over its quota.
  It deleted fixed batches of 1,000 entries, so missing the 80 % target by a few bytes after one
  batch deleted a whole second one; whether a workspace kept its entries or lost all of them
  depended on payload sizes (the length of the workspace's absolute path). Each step now deletes
  about as many least recently used entries as the excess needs, at most 1,000.
- The index cache's best-effort WAL checkpoint no longer waits on readers: a `TRUNCATE`
  checkpoint blocks new writers while it waits, so under the 5 s busy timeout another process
  sharing the workspace's cache could fail its writes with `SQLITE_BUSY`.
